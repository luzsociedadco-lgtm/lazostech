## Project title

LazosTech UniMarket

## tagline

A verified circular marketplace for university communities

## Project description

UniMarket is a circular marketplace for university students. It helps students buy, sell, or exchange books, technology, clothing, and campus-based microbusiness products inside their own community.

The experience is designed around trust and local coordination: student verification, clear seller information, campus pickup points, and a lightweight request flow before any exchange is confirmed. This keeps useful items in circulation, reduces friction between students, and keeps value within the university community.

LazosTech is an initiative driven by LuzSociedad. The pilot narrative is grounded in validation with 35 Universidad del Valle students, not a claim of mass adoption.

## What we built

- Public UniMarket demo with no login required for judges.
- Search and category filters for campus listings.
- Verified listing cards with seller, price, exchange mode, and pickup location.
- Request flow for coordinating a safe campus exchange.
- Draft listing flow for students who want to offer an item.
- Community impact panel showing the circular-pilot model.
