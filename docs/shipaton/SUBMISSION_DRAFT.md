## One-line summary

UniMarket is a verified university marketplace for buying, selling, donating,
and exchanging items within a trusted student community.

## Project overview

University students often need affordable items while other students already
have those items available. UniMarket turns that mismatch into a safer,
community-first marketplace with verified listings, direct contact, and a
clear exchange flow.

The current public demo is available at:

https://www.lazostech.com/unimarket

The demo shows category browsing, featured listings, listing creation, search,
item details, and preparation of an exchange request.
